# Resilience Watch

A daily open-source intelligence briefing on threats that could disrupt power, water,
communications, fuel, payments, food or medical supply in the US and Alabama — and what
to do about them. Live at **https://szk5334.github.io/ResilienceWatch/**.

An automated research agent runs every morning, follows [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md), writes
`data/latest.json`, and pushes. The page renders that file.

Levels: 1 Normal · 2 Guarded · 3 Elevated · 4 High · 5 Severe. Definitions and tripwires are in the protocol.
